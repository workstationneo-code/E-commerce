# Electrical E-commerce

A bilingual (Arabic/English) electrical products storefront for retail and business customers.

## Project direction
- B2C and B2B shopping experiences
- Arabic and English (RTL/LTR)
- EGP and SYP display currencies
- Customer storefront and administration area
- Product catalogue, search, cart, and quotation workflows

## Development status
Initial scaffold in progress. Payment processing, authentication, inventory persistence, and production administration require configured backend services and credentials before they can be enabled.
